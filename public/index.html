How ordering works

The order line answers in your language, day or night.

  1. Give your customer number

    Say your six-digit customer number, or the name of your business.

  2. Say what you need

    Brands, pack sizes and number of cases. Ask what’s available if you’re not sure.

  3. Get your order number

    You hear the order read back, the estimated total and your delivery day.
